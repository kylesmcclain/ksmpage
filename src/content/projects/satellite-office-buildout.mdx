## Approach

The site was designed to be a full member of the corporate network from the first day rather than
a remote annex with workarounds. Fortinet firewalling and switching were deployed to give the
office a real perimeter, and an IPSEC site-to-site VPN tunnel established back to headquarters so
that internal resources resolved and reached the same way they did at the main site.

The schedule risk was external, as it usually is. ISP circuit activation was driven directly rather
than waited on, and the cabling vendor was coordinated against the build schedule so that
termination work and equipment installation did not collide.

## Result

The site was delivered ahead of schedule and functional on day one — no interim connectivity, no
temporary access arrangements, and no gap between the office opening and the office working.
